## Brand & Style

FitBento embodies the Japanese philosophy of *ichiju-sansai* (one soup, three dishes)—balanced, intentional, and nourishing. The aesthetic pairs the meticulous spatial balance of modern Japanese interior architecture with the fresh, organic vitality of culinary wellness. It rejects the sterile, clinical appearance of conventional calorie counters in favor of warm tactile minimalism, mindful clarity, and serene structure.

The target audience consists of mindful eaters, meal preppers, and fitness enthusiasts who value sustainable, whole-food nutrition over aggressive dieting tropes. Visual rhythm evokes artisanal ceramics, steaming matcha bowls, and organized wooden bento dividers. The interface provides quiet confidence, visual breathing room, and clear nutritional legibility without sensory overload.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in culinary freshness and earthen warmth. The primary matcha spectrum provides visual hierarchy and focal anchoring, complemented by roasted terracotta accents for high-priority alerts and macro distinctions.

- **Primary (Matcha Core):**
  - Deep Matcha (`#2D5A27`): Dominant brand color, primary calls-to-action, active navigational states, and high-emphasis display type.
  - Steeped Leaf (`#4E8746`): Interactive hover states, secondary accents, and focused borders.
  - Young Sprout (`#87A96B`): Carbohydrate indicators, non-critical highlights, and balanced metric fills.
  - Matcha Mist (`#E8F0E4`): Soft interactive container backgrounds, active tab pills, and low-contrast selection fills.

- **Secondary & Tertiary (Culinary Accents):**
  - Roasted Persimmon (`#C26D48`): Protein metrics, urgent reminders, and primary destructive confirmation.
  - Golden Sesame (`#D4A359`): Lipid/fat macro rings, dynamic streaks, and warning notices.
  - Ocean Nori (`#1C2E20`): High-contrast text, primary headers, and chart axes.

- **Neutrals (Washi Paper & Woodgrain):**
  - Base Canvas (`#FBF8F2`): Full-viewport background offering warm, non-glare reading.
  - Bento Surface (`#F5EFE6`): Primary card, panel, and sheet surfaces.
  - Inset Well (`#EFE6D8`): Recessed containers, table headers, and inactive progress tracks.
  - Ceramic Border (`#E2D7C3`): Subtle structural dividers mimicking fine pottery seams.
  - Sumi Text (`#242622`): Primary body text, highly legible with low eye fatigue.
  - Stone Text (`#6E7268`): Secondary metadata, timestamps, and placeholder copy.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all typographic tiers. Its rounded geometric construction, wide apertures, and clean terminal cuts balance modern functionality with warm, organic harmony. 

Numerical meal counts, calorie figures, and macro weights rely on `data-metric` with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifting during real-time calorie logging and slider operations. Display scales enforce negative letter-spacing for tight visual cadence, while `label-sm` applies subtle letter-spacing for micro-scale legibility on macro tags and dietary indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural grid of a lacquer bento box: compartmentalized, intentional, and modular. Surfaces conform to a strict structural baseline while maintaining generous breathing room.

- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column layout with fixed max-width centered at `1280px`. Features a persistent 280px left navigation dock, asymmetric split containers for dashboard metrics, and a dedicated 360px contextual meal prep rail. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Navigation folds into a floating glass-dock or collapsable side rail. Meal prep breakdowns collapse into two equal-width modular stacks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Bento grid cells reflow vertically into tactile swipe cards. Bottom navigation provides one-thumb access to quick logging and the Mifflin-St Jeor intake recalculator.

Internal card compartments utilize `space-md` for standard separation, while grouping related nutrition facts uses compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, tea-tinted ambient shadows and subtle tonal paper-stacking rather than stark grey drop shadows.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#FBF8F2` background.
- **Level 1 (Bento Compartments & Modules):** Solid `#F5EFE6` surface layered over canvas, delineated by a soft border (`1px solid #E2D7C3`) and an ultra-diffused warm ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(78, 55, 30, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Active Cards):** Elevated bento card lift: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(78, 55, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Delivery Popovers & Dropdowns):** Floating meal builder modals: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 46, 32, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(78, 55, 30, 0.06)`, framed with a crisp inner highlight border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Recessed / Inset Tiers:** Macro progress bar tracks and input wells sit sunken into the card, styled with a background of `#EFE6D8` and an inset shadow: `inset 0 2px 4px 0 rgba(78, 55, 30, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs `roundedness: 2` (base `0.5rem` / `8px`, containers `1rem` / `16px`, parent cards `1.5rem` / `24px`). This creates a soft, organic feel akin to hand-carved cedar bento boxes and river-smoothed stones.

- **Macro Badges & Pill Tabs:** Fully rounded capsule shapes (`9999px`) provide ergonomic touch targets for toggles, status pills, and filters.
- **Bento Cells & Dashboard Tiles:** Standardized at `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`), establishing defined modular compartments that cluster food groups cleanly.
- **Inputs & Button Actions:** Set to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) for reliable target acquisition and soft-touch visual appeal.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#2D5A27` fill, crisp white text, `1rem` border radius, horizontal padding of `1.25rem`, vertical padding of `0.75rem`. Hover triggers a smooth shift to `#4E8746` accompanied by an elevation lift to Level 2.
- **Secondary:** Filled with `#E8F0E4`, `#2D5A27` typography, no outer border. Hover transitions to `#D9E8D3`.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #E2D7C3`, `#242622` text. Hover adds a `#F5EFE6` wash with border darkening to `#87A96B`.

### Bento Calorie Breakdown Cards
Compartmentalized layout tiles built on `#F5EFE6` surfaces with `1.5rem` radii. The card interior divides naturally into:
- Header zone: meal label (e.g., "Asa-gohan / Breakfast"), schedule stamp, and consumed/target calorie split.
- Center section: high-contrast food item rows featuring dish imagery, weight in grams, and calorie badges.
- Bottom shelf: micro nutrient distribution bar with proportional segments for Protein, Carbs, and Fats.

### Nutrient Macro Donut & Progress Bars
- **Donut Visualizers:** Stroke width of `10px` with rounded end-caps. Tracks use recessed `#EFE6D8`. Concentric or segmented rings utilize Deep Matcha (`#2D5A27`) for target calories, Persimmon (`#C26D48`) for protein, Sprout (`#87A96B`) for carbs, and Golden Sesame (`#D4A359`) for fats. Center hole features primary calorie tally in `data-metric`.
- **Linear Progress Meters:** Height of `8px`, track background `#EFE6D8`, with smooth spring-physics fills terminating in fully rounded caps.

### Mifflin-St Jeor Interactive Calculator Pill Tabs
- Pill-shaped switcher encased in a recessed track (`#EFE6D8`, `p-1`, `rounded-full`).
- Active pill transitions via a floating `#FFFFFF` or `#2D5A27` island with an ambient Level 1 shadow, clearly distinguishing biological sex, formula constants, and expenditure activity tiers (Sedentary, Light, Moderate, Heavy).
- Range sliders within the calculator feature a `#2D5A27` thumb (24px diameter) over a `#87A96B` active track.

### Delivery Auto-Logger Status Badge
- Capsule component (`rounded-full`, `px-3`, `py-1`) signaling meal kit synchronization.
- **Synced / Logged:** `#E8F0E4` background, `#2D5A27` text, with a pulsing inner green dot (`#4E8746`).
- **Transit / In-Route:** `#FDF4E7` background, `#D4A359` text, accompanied by a small van or bento box glyph.
- **Action Required:** `#FBECE8` background, `#C26D48` text, actionable on click to reconcile missing nutritional telemetry.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Background `#FBF8F2`, border `1.5px solid #E2D7C3`, border-radius `0.75rem`. Focus ring adopts `#2D5A27` with a 3px soft tint glow (`rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth squared-circle forms (`rounded-md` and `rounded-full`) with `#2D5A27` checked state featuring a crisp white interior icon. Inactive track matches `#E2D7C3`.
